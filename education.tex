\begin{itemize}

	\item Designed a forwarding strategy using Public Key Encryption with Keyword Search (PEKS) to protect content names while preserving forwarding functionality.
	\item Implemented searchable-encryption primitives using the Pairing-Based Cryptography (PBC) library in C++.
	\item Designed, implemented, and benchmarked the PEKS-based forwarding strategy and evaluated cryptographic and networking performance.
	\item Extended the scheme to two-tier network topologies and analyzed the effect of trapdoor placement on cryptographic processing overhead.
	\item Research resulted in two peer-reviewed publications and ongoing citations in the field.
\end{itemize}
