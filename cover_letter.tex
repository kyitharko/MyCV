I am writing to submit an open application to F-Secure. I would be excited to contribute in a cybersecurity research, privacy engineering, secure software development, or technical education role where my applied cryptography, networking, and Linux systems background can support security products that protect people in their everyday digital lives.

I hold a Ph.D. in Engineering from Kanazawa University, Japan, where my research focused on privacy and security in Named Data Networking. My doctoral work involved designing, implementing, and evaluating a privacy-preserving forwarding strategy based on Public Key Encryption with Keyword Search (PEKS). I implemented pairing-based cryptographic primitives in C++ using the PBC library, integrated them into the networking strategy, and benchmarked the resulting security and performance trade-offs. This research resulted in two peer-reviewed publications.

I am currently reimplementing this work in Rust using BLS12-381 and the arkworks ecosystem. Moving the original construction from symmetric to asymmetric pairings has required careful reasoning about group placement, pairing equations, implementation correctness, and protocol behavior. The implementation includes the core PEKS operations, RFC 9380 hash-to-curve functionality, and automated correctness tests.

My broader background includes Linux systems, computer networking, distributed systems, network security, packet-level analysis, network forensics, and system-level troubleshooting. As a university lecturer, I taught Network Defense and Network Forensics, including intrusion detection, firewall design, traffic investigation, and evidence interpretation. As the technical lead for an e-commerce platform, I make infrastructure and security decisions, operate secure web hosting and TLS, and troubleshoot production systems. These experiences have taught me to connect careful security reasoning with practical user and operational needs.

F-Secure’s focus on making digital life safer is especially meaningful to me because my research has consistently centered on privacy and security at the point where people and networked systems interact. I am comfortable learning unfamiliar technologies from papers and specifications, working across research and implementation, documenting decisions, and explaining complex security topics clearly. I would welcome a conversation about where my security research, C++/Rust, networking, teaching, and hands-on infrastructure experience could be most useful to F-Secure.
