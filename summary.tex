Experienced in designing, implementing, and evaluating privacy-preserving protocols using Public Key Encryption with Keyword Search (PEKS) and pairing-based cryptography, with research implementations in C++ and Rust. Strong background in Linux, computer networking, protocol analysis, security, and research engineering, with two peer-reviewed publications and experience translating academic designs into working implementations.
